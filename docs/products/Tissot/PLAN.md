# Tissot T-Complication Squelette

Visual reconstruction of reference **T070.405.16.411.00**. The reference pack was supplied in the adjacent `Tissot_watch` folder. The model uses metres and faces −Y in Blender, +Z in glTF.

| Evidence | Application |
| --- | --- |
| Observed in the supplied front and detail photos | Round brushed steel case, projecting fluted crown, dark chapter ring, blue appliqués and hands, offset seconds at nine, exposed gold-coloured wheels, ruby details and openworked bridges. |
| Observed in the supplied rear and strap photos | Exhibition window, steel winding wheels, clasp, black stitched leather and a curved display pose. |
| Supplied reference-pack dimensions | Nominal case diameter 43 mm, thickness 11.99 mm and lug opening 22 mm. The ETA technical communication gives 36.60 mm and 4.50 mm for the standard 6497-1 movement. |
| Inferred from the ETA 6497-1 technical communication and parts imagery | Manual winding path, barrel-to-escapement relationships and jewel roles. Bridge outlines, depths, the clasp internals and hidden support details are estimated. |
| Illustrative | Tooth geometry/counts, spring and pallet motion, extraction offsets, stylized strap texture and the staged guide. Measured case and movement interfaces would be needed for a manufacturing reconstruction. |

The front photo guides the three projecting arms of the openworked dial-side plate at 5–6, 7–9, and 9–10 o’clock. The upper-left arm supports the subsidiary seconds pivot. Satin bridge faces, darker cut walls and perimeter, and brass wheels distinguish the depth layers.

**Run Cycle** advances the going train and hands at 60× watch time. At viewer speed 1× the small-seconds hand completes one revolution per playback second. The balance and pallet retain the readable 2.5 Hz oscillation, so the display combines two time scales. Gear ratios are representative. **Manual Winding** demonstrates the winding train separately. The staged take-apart positions are display poses.
